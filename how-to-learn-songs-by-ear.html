How to Learn Songs by Ear

Tired of searching for inaccurate chord charts and tabs on the internet? Training yourself to learn songs by ear is the most liberating thing you can do as a musician.

Why tabs and chord charts hold you back

When you read music, your eyes are doing the work, not your ears. This bypasses the musical part of your brain. By learning songs by ear, you internalize the music, remember it longer, and improve your overall musicianship.

The 3-Step Transcription Process

  1. Find the Bass (Root): The bass guitar usually plays the root note of the chord. Use your ear to find this single note on your instrument.
  2. Identify the Quality: Listen to the harmony above the bass. Is it Major (happy)? Minor (sad)? Dominant (bluesy)?
  3. Find the Melody: Use interval recognition to trace the distance between the melody notes.

How BananaTone helps
