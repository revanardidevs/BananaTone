Musical Dictation Practice

Musical dictation is the act of listening to a piece of music and writing it down on staff paper. It is the ultimate test of a musician's ear, requiring fast interval recognition and strong pitch memory.

The challenge of dictation

In a dictation exam, the music doesn't stop. You must instantly recognize the distance between notes while simultaneously memorizing the rhythm. If your interval recognition is slow, you will fall behind and fail.

Building speed

To get better at dictation, you don't need to do full dictations — you need to isolate your weakness. For most students, the weakness is interval recognition speed.

Use BananaTone's Interval mode on Fast Speed with Auto-Next turned on. This forces your brain to process the intervals instantly, mimicking the pressure of a real dictation environment.

Harmonic Dictation

For harmonic dictation (writing down chords), switch to BananaTone's Chord mode. Start with triads, and drill them until you can instantly tell if the professor played a Major, Minor, or Diminished chord just by its color.
