About RabbitEar

A clean, fast, and completely free ear training tool for musicians of all levels.

Our Mission

RabbitEar was built out of a simple frustration: most ear training tools are either too complicated, require paid subscriptions, or look like they were built in the 1990s. We wanted something better.

Our goal is to provide a friction-free, distraction-free environment where you can drop in for 5 minutes of practice and leave with a slightly better ear than yesterday. No logins, no paywalls, just you and the music.

Why "RabbitEar"?

Rabbits are known for their exceptional hearing. Their large ears act as acoustic funnels, allowing them to detect predators from incredible distances. While we can't promise you'll hear a fox from two miles away, we hope RabbitEar helps you develop a similarly acute sense of pitch and harmony in music.
