How to practice

Practice small, repeat often.

Start with a small answer pool. When your accuracy is stable, move to Balanced or Challenge.

Intervals

Listen to two notes. First learn obvious contrasts like Minor 3rd, Major 3rd, Perfect 5th, and Octave.

Chords

Start with Major vs Minor. Use replay and compare when the color feels unclear.

Notes

Note recognition is hard. Use a small pool first. The octave changes, but the pitch class answer stays the same.

Scales

Listen for the color of the scale. Do not try to memorize every note at full speed.

20-minute routine

5 minutes intervals, 5 minutes chords, 5 minutes notes or scales, then 5 minutes weak areas.

Comfort

Use moderate volume. Ear training improves through clear, relaxed listening, not loud volume.
