Ear Training for Music Producers

In modern music production, speed is everything. Ear training gives you the ability to hear a sample, instantly know its key and scale, and immediately start writing basslines and melodies that fit perfectly.

The Producer's Secret Weapon

Many producers rely on MIDI chord packs or trial-and-error to find notes that work. This slows down the creative process and leads to generic-sounding beats.

By training your ear to recognize scales and chords, you can write original melodies that perfectly match the emotional vibe of your track, entirely from scratch.

Working with Samples

When you flip a sample, you need to know its harmonic content to add 808s or synths on top.

Practice Scale Recognition in BananaTone (especially Natural Minor and Minor Pentatonic) so you can instantly recognize the "flavor" of a sample and know exactly which notes you are allowed to use in your piano roll.

Tuning your 808s

One of the most common mistakes in beatmaking is out-of-tune 808 basslines. Because 808s are so low in frequency, pitch is hard to hear. Use BananaTone's Note Recognition tool to build solid pitch memory, so an out-of-tune sub-bass will instantly sound wrong to you.
