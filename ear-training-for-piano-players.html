Ear Training for Piano Players

The piano is the ultimate harmonic instrument, capable of playing 10 notes at once. To master it, your ears need to be as agile as your fingers.

Beyond Sheet Music

Classical piano training often emphasizes sight-reading over aural skills. While reading is important, playing entirely from sheet music limits your ability to improvise, play by ear, or join a jam session.

Ear training allows you to hear a chord progression and instantly map it to the keyboard.

Focus on Chords and Harmony

Because pianists are often responsible for the entire harmonic foundation of a song, Chord Ear Training is your most important tool.

  • Start by drilling Major vs Minor triads.
  • Move on to 7th chords (Major 7, Minor 7, Dominant 7) which are essential for jazz and modern pop.
  • Practice identifying the highest note (the melody) and lowest note (the bass) of block chords.

The advantage of the keyboard layout

Pianists have a massive advantage: the piano is a linear map of music theory. When you hear a Perfect 5th, you can easily visualize the 7 semitone gap. Use BananaTone's interval and chord trainers to connect your ears directly to this visual map.
