20-Minute Ear Training Routine for Musicians

Consistency is the key to a great musical ear. You don't need to practice for hours—just 20 minutes of highly focused, daily practice will yield incredible results.

Minute 0–3: warm up with notes

Start your session by waking up your ears. Use a functional ear training approach: listen to a drone or a chord progression establishing a key, and then try to identify the scale degree (1 through 7) of a random note.

This builds your tonal memory and helps you hear how notes feel relative to a key center.

Minute 3–8: intervals

Spend 5 minutes strictly on intervals. If you are a beginner, stick to a small pool like Major 2nd, Perfect 4th, and Perfect 5th. Listen to both ascending and descending intervals.

Singing the interval back before you answer is a highly effective way to internalize the sound.

Minute 8–13: chords

Now shift to harmony. Spend 5 minutes identifying chord qualities. Start by distinguishing between Major and Minor triads. Once you are comfortable, add Diminished and Augmented chords to the mix.

Try listening for the third of the chord to tell if it's major or minor.

Minute 13–17: scales

Spend 4 minutes identifying scales. Can you tell the difference between a Major scale and a Natural Minor scale? What about the Dorian mode?

Listen for the characteristic notes (like the flat 3rd in a minor scale) to quickly identify what you are hearing.

Minute 17–20: review weak areas

Use the last 3 minutes to review your mistakes from the session. BananaTone automatically tracks your weak areas. Focus exclusively on the intervals or chords you struggled with today.
