Functional Ear Training

Functional ear training is a highly effective method where you learn to recognize notes not by their distance from the previous note, but by their 'function' or 'gravity' in relation to the key center.

What is Functional Ear Training?

Standard interval training teaches you to hear C to E as a "Major 3rd."

Functional ear training teaches you to hear E as the "3rd degree" of the C Major scale. Every note in a key has a specific feeling of tension or resolution pulling it toward the tonic (the root note).

The gravity of notes

  • The Tonic (Root/Do): Ultimate rest. Zero tension.
  • The 5th (Sol): Very stable, supports the root.
  • The 3rd (Mi): Peaceful, determines major/minor color.
  • The 7th (Ti): Extreme tension, desperately wants to pull up to the tonic.

How to practice functionally

In BananaTone, go to the Notes mode and select the Beginner or Balanced preset. Before you guess the note, try to feel its "gravity." Does it feel like it's resting at home (C), or does it feel tense and wanting to move (B)?
